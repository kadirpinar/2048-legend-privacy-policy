2048 Legend — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

EnglishTürkçe

2048 Legend ("the App") is developed by Kadir Pınar ("we", "the developer"). This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Summary: The App does not ask you for your name, email, or an account. Your game progress is stored on your device. For ads and purchases, third-party services (Google AdMob, RevenueCat, Apple/Google) process limited technical data.

1. Data We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information (name, email, phone, etc.) from you, and we do not require you to create an account. Third-party services within the App may process the following:

DataPurposeProcessed by
Device identifiers (e.g. advertising ID / IDFA)Ad delivery and measurementGoogle AdMob
Usage / interaction dataAd and performance analyticsGoogle AdMob
Diagnostics (crash / performance) dataImproving app stabilityGoogle AdMob
Purchase history (with an anonymous user ID)Subscription / purchase management and restoreRevenueCat, Apple/Google

This data is not linked to your identity (name/email/account).

2. Tracking Permission (iOS only — App Tracking Transparency)

On iOS, before using your device advertising identifier (IDFA) to show personalized ads, we ask for your permission. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. You can change this choice anytime in your device settings.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Your game progress, scores, settings, and in-game currency/items are stored locally on your device only. Deleting the App removes this data from your device.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following services, whose data practices are governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — purchases are processed by the respective store.

5. Ads and Purchases

The App is free and contains ads. Optionally, a Premium subscription that removes ads and in-game currency (coins/gems) packs are offered. Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we do not have access to your card/payment details.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your Rights

Under KVKK (Türkiye) and GDPR (EU), you have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your data. You can turn off ad personalization in your device settings and remove your local data by deleting the App. For requests, contact us at the email below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates are published on this page and the "Last updated" date is revised.

9. Contact

For privacy questions: kadirp97@gmail.com
